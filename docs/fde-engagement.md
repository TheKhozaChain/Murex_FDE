# FDE Engagement — HarbourView Bank

HarbourView Bank is a fictional capital-markets institution with recurring daily-reporting exceptions. Analysts currently assemble evidence from batch status, report populations, trades, market data, logs, runbooks, change records, and stakeholder conversations. The discovery shows that the expensive part is not a single diagnostic query; it is coordinating fragmented evidence and accountable decisions under time pressure.

Stakeholders disagree about who sees incidents first, whether manual controls are reliable, and whether materiality is consistent. The engagement treats these conflicts as workflow-design inputs. It prioritises deterministic evidence collection and bounded synthesis while leaving production actions and risk acceptance with humans.

The deployment plan is shadow mode, analyst-assist, controlled pilot by incident class, then expansion only after safety and adoption gates pass. Adoption measures include acceptance/rejection reasons, investigation time, escalation accuracy, knowledge reuse, and backlog movement. The value case applies conservative volume, labour-cost, and realisation assumptions and clearly labels simulated versus projected values.
